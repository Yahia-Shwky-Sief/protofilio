With You

With You is an app I built between 2022 and 2023 to support refugees in Egypt. Palestinians, Syrians, and Sudanese people living in Egypt are officially treated as "guests" rather than refugees, which means they receive no governmental support of any kind. With You was designed to help fill that gap and give these communities some of the support the system doesn't provide.

The project won the AFFIAT finals, confirming the problem was real and our solution had value. Unfortunately, it never went into production, but it taught me how much a good idea depends on resources and support to reach the people who need it.

Robobekia

Robobekia is an e-commerce application with a unique business model. It lets users sell recyclable waste, which the system then collects and recycles in an organized way.

The system serves all stakeholders through three connected applications:

  • Customer App: photograph and categorize waste, then submit a sale request.
  • Driver App: guides drivers to collect waste from customers' locations.
  • Admin Dashboard: track orders, manage users, and monitor collection and pricing.

The project brings e-commerce, environmental sustainability, and smart technologies together in one experience.

2026

Egycar

Egycar is a ride-hailing platform I built for the dense towns and rural areas of Egypt that big apps ignore. About 62 million people live outside Cairo, Alexandria, and the other major cities, including my hometown, Dessouk, which has 400,000 residents and no ride-hailing service.

Existing apps skip these places because their software and per-ride costs don't work with fares of 20 to 60 US cents. Egycar was designed around those prices from the start. Everything is built locally, with no external APIs except online payments through a local company, paid in Egyptian currency. That keeps costs low enough to make small-town rides economically viable.
